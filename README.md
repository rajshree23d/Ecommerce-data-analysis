## 🎯 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset** to understand **sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, seller performance, and delivery outcomes**.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow — from **data profiling and cleaning to SQL analysis, customer segmentation, statistical testing, and business insights**.

---

## 🏢 Business Context

The Olist dataset contains approximately **100,000 e-commerce orders** along with customer, seller, product, payment, review, and delivery information.

The objective is to uncover meaningful patterns and insights that can help businesses better understand:

📈 Revenue & Sales Performance
👥 Customer Behavior & Value
📦 Product & Category Performance
🚚 Delivery Performance
⭐ Customer Satisfaction
🏪 Seller Performance

---

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

| 🔹 Area                     | 📌 Analysis                                                        |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 🧹 **Data Cleaning**        | Missing values, duplicates, data types & quality checks            |
| 📊 **EDA**                  | Revenue trends, categories, customers & delivery                   |
| 💻 **SQL Analysis**         | Joins, CTEs, aggregations & window functions                       |
| 👥 **RFM Analysis**         | Customer segmentation based on Recency, Frequency & Monetary value |
| 📅 **Cohort Analysis**      | Customer retention by acquisition month                            |
| 📈 **Statistical Analysis** | Delivery performance vs. customer reviews                          |
| 🏪 **Seller Analysis**      | Seller performance and tiering                                     |
| 🚨 **Outlier Detection**    | IQR & Z-score methodology                                          |
| 📊 **Dashboarding**         | Business KPIs and insights using Power BI                          |

---

## 💡 Key Insights

> 🏆 **Top 3 categories** contribute approximately **45% of total revenue**

> 🚚 Approximately **92% of orders** were delivered on time

> ⭐ Late delivery was associated with an approximately **1.2-point lower review score**

> 💰 **Champions represent 8% of customers** but contribute approximately **35% of revenue**
